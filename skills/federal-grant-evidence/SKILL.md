# Federal Grant Evidence

Use `search_federal_grants` when the user needs to discover a current U.S. federal opportunity from an official public source.

Use `analyze_federal_grant` after one opportunity is known and the user wants a cited preview of applicant type, country, deadline, cost-share, or requested-amount gates.

Use `get_federal_grant_evidence_pack` only when the user explicitly wants the complete versioned hard-gate and award-history pack. Reuse the same `request_id` for retries of the same request.

Keep `PASS`, `FAIL`, and `UNKNOWN` distinct. Do not present the result as legal eligibility, project-relevance scoring, an award prediction, application writing, or submission. All three tools are free during the limited beta and must not request a wallet or payment authorization.
